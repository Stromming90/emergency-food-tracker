Emergency food & water at home — one simple recommendation

Aim to be self-sufficient for at least one week (ideally two) per person:

  • ~2,200 kcal of food per person per day — about 15 kg of shelf-stable food per person for two weeks
  • 3–5 liters of water per person per day (drinking + cooking + basic hygiene) — around 40–60 L per person for two weeks

What to stock

Food that keeps at room temperature, fills you up, and needs no (or minimal) cooking — e.g.:

  • oats
  • pasta
  • rice
  • crispbread
  • nuts
  • dried fruit
  • tinned meat/fish
  • beans/lentils
  • canned vegetables and fruit
  • peanut butter
  • UHT milk
  • oil
  • salt
  • honey
  • bottled water

How to manage it

Buy a little extra of what you normally eat, rotate it in daily life, check dates every six months, and adapt for children, special diets, and pets.

⚙️ Settings

Welcome to the Settings menu. What you can do here depends on your role:

  • General — this overview.
  • Users — create and manage user accounts. Global admins manage everyone; household admins manage the users of the households they administer (create accounts, reset passwords, grant or revoke household admin).
  • Households — create new households (you become the household admin of a household you create), see members, migrate inventory between households (household admins), and delete households (global admin only).
  • Types & Categories — browse the product types and category options used in the dropdowns — now a tab on the main page (global admin only, editing behind an Edit button).
  • Authentication — configure OIDC / SSO login (Keycloak, Authentik, Authelia…), and enable/disable SSO or password login. Global admin only.
  • Notifications — enable real push notifications on each device with one tap (Settings → Notifications). No third-party apps, no accounts, no tokens: the server generates its own keys and your browser's push service delivers the alerts — even when the app is closed. Each user manages their own devices.
  • API Key — your personal key for the gethomepage widget. Each user has their own key and can regenerate it at any time; the widget only reports the households you have access to.

Roles: a global admin can do and see everything. A household admin can edit the product list, migrate inventory and manage the users of the households they administer, and can make other users household admins of those households.
